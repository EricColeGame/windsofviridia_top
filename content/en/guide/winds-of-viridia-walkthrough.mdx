## The Four Systems Every Route Depends On

Shark Jump has described a game built on four interlocking pillars. Understanding them early saves hours of backtracking later.

### 1. Freeform Movement

Rolling, sliding, flipping, and grappling are all part of the traversal kit, and the world is described as seamless. That combination usually means the fastest path between two points is rarely the obvious one. Practice chaining moves in open areas before you need them in a fight.

Strange plants are the second half of the movement system. Some launch you higher, some throw you farther, and some open routes to deeper areas. Treat every odd-looking plant as a potential shortcut rather than scenery.

### 2. Agile Combat

Enemies are "frenzied," which in practice means aggressive and unpredictable. The combat loop leans on speed rather than blocking, so stamina management and positioning matter more than raw damage. Learn each creature's wind-up animation and you'll spend far less time healing.

### 3. Totem Customization

Lost relics and treasures can be equipped as totems and combined to reshape your playstyle. This is the deepest system in the game and the one most likely to change between announcement and launch, so plan in terms of goals rather than specific items.

### 4. Companion Support

A Mothbug companion assists in both exploration and combat. Companions in this genre usually double as traversal tools, so expect the Mothbug to unlock access you can't get on your own.

| System | What to Practice First | Why It Pays Off |
|---|---|---|
| Movement | Chaining roll, slide, and grapple | Shortens every return trip |
| Plants | Testing each plant type once | Reveals hidden vertical routes |
| Combat | Reading enemy wind-ups | Fewer deaths to frenzied packs |
| Totems | Swapping combos at safe spots | Finds your preferred damage profile |
| Mothbug | Using it in every new biome | Surfaces optional discoveries |

## Boss Preparation: What to Expect From Grand Bosses

The announcement names three broad boss categories. Specific names aren't public yet, so treat the table below as a preparation framework rather than a boss list.

| Boss Archetype | What It Suggests | Prep Focus |
|---|---|---|

| Living statues | Armored, slower, likely resistant to brute force | Look for weak points and environmental tools |

Across all three, the pattern is the same: survive the opening phase, learn the tell, then commit to damage. Players who treat bosses as puzzles rather than damage checks tend to clear them far faster.

A practical tip that applies to nearly every action-adventure boss: enter the arena with full consumables and at least one totem combo you haven't tried yet. If your main build stalls, you'll have a backup plan already equipped.
