### Traversal as a difficulty layer

Seamless worlds with grapple mechanics tend to hide their hardest challenges off the critical path: grottos, ruins, and vertical glades that reward clean movement. Historically, this is where players report the sharpest spikes — not in boss rooms, but in optional platforming that demands a specific sequence of inputs.
