## Winds of Viridia System Requirements at a Glance

Two things jump out immediately. Keep in mind that the game is not yet available and is listed with a planned release date of Q1 2027. Specs published this far ahead of launch can change, so treat this table as a snapshot rather than gospel.

## Minimum vs. Recommended: What the Two Tiers Actually Mean

Steam doesn't state a target resolution or frame rate for either tier, so you have to read between the lines. In practice, a minimum tier usually means the game will boot and remain playable at lower settings, while the recommended tier is the configuration the developers used for a smoother, more stable experience.

Here's how the two columns differ in real terms:

| Change | Minimum → Recommended | Practical Effect |
|---|---|---|
| CPU generation | i5-3470 (Ivy Bridge, 2012) → i5-6600K (Skylake, 2015) | Fewer hitches in busy combat and dense foliage |
| RAM | 3 GB → 5 GB | More headroom for streaming a seamless open world |
| GPU | GTX 1050 Ti → GTX 1070 | Meaningfully higher frame rates at 1080p |
| VRAM | 3 GB → 5 GB | Room for higher-resolution textures |
| Storage | 5 GB → 5 GB | Identical; install size shouldn't scale with settings |

The RAM jump from 3 GB to 5 GB is the most telling line in the whole table. A seamless, handcrafted world with fast rolling, sliding, and grappling traversal needs memory for streaming assets in the background. If your machine sits at exactly 4 GB of RAM, you're in an awkward middle ground — technically above the minimum, but short of the recommended figure.

## Breaking Down Each Winds of Viridia System Requirement

### Processor

The minimum i5-3470 is a quad-core desktop chip from 2012, and the recommended i5-6600K is its 2015 successor. Any modern 6-core CPU — from either AMD or Intel — comfortably clears both bars. If you're on a laptop with a low-wattage dual-core processor, however, expect the CPU to be your first bottleneck rather than the graphics card.

### Graphics Card and VRAM

The GTX 1050 Ti listed as the minimum is a 4 GB-class card in most retail versions, but the Steam listing specifies a 3 GB VRAM variant. The recommended GTX 1070 carries 5 GB here. Because the game leans on a stylized, handcrafted art direction rather than photoreal detail, a card in the 1050 Ti range should still produce a presentable image at 1080p with settings dialed back.

### Memory

3 GB of RAM as a minimum is unusually frugal — Windows 10 alone can consume a large share of that at idle. In practice, most players will want 8 GB or more so the game isn't competing with the operating system and background apps. Treat the listed 3 GB as a floor, not a target.

### Storage

5 GB of available space is the easiest requirement to satisfy. Even a modest SATA SSD has room to spare. That said, an SSD will noticeably improve load times and asset streaming compared to a mechanical drive, especially when grappling across large areas.

### OS and DirectX

Windows 10 version 21H1 (build 19043) or newer is the baseline, which means Windows 11 is fine. DirectX 12 support is required, and every GPU listed above supports it. If you're still on an older Windows 10 build, run Windows Update before launch — this is the single most common cause of "the game won't start" complaints.

Finally, revisit the Winds of Viridia system requirements closer to launch. Publishers frequently revise specs during the final months of development, and a Q1 2027 window means there's plenty of time for the numbers to move in either direction.

## FAQ

No. A GTX 1050 Ti, 3 GB of RAM, and 5 GB of storage place this firmly in lightweight territory compared to most modern releases. Almost any gaming PC built in the last several years should clear the minimum tier.

Probably, if your laptop has a dedicated GPU and at least 8 GB of RAM. Integrated-graphics laptops are a gamble until benchmarks appear, and Steam Deck compatibility is still listed as unknown.

**Do I need a controller to play?**
The developers recommend one, and Xbox controllers are officially supported, but a keyboard and mouse setup should still work. The game is listed as playable without quick-time events, which helps on any input method.

**Will the specs change before release?**
They might. The game is not yet available and is planned for Q1 2027, so the published Winds of Viridia system requirements could be adjusted. Wishlisting the game on Steam is the easiest way to catch any updates.
