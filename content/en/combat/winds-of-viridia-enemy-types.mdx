## How to Read Enemy Telegraphs Before They Land

Most deaths in this genre are not damage problems — they are attention problems. Telegraphs are the visual and audio language the designers use to warn you, and they are consistent by design.

| Cue Type | What It Signals | Your Response |
|---|---|---|
| Wind-up pose / limb pull-back | Attack incoming in a fixed direction | Sidestep, don't backpedal |
| Glow or color flash | Charged or unblockable hit | Dodge, not block |
| Audio sting | Imminent commit, often off-screen | Reset spacing immediately |
| Ground marker or shadow | Delayed area attack | Leave the marked zone early |
| Post-attack slump | Recovery window | This is your damage phase |
| Sudden silence | Phase change or summon | Reposition and heal |

Three habits make telegraph reading automatic:

- **Fight the first encounter defensively.** No damage racing. Just watch.
- **Play with sound.** Audio stings routinely fire before the animation finishes.
- **Count the combo.** If an enemy swings three times, the fourth beat is usually the opening.

## Common Mistakes Players Make Against New Rosters

New rosters punish assumptions. These are the patterns that cost the most retries, and they apply to nearly any enemy set.

1. **Backpedaling instead of sidestepping.** Most attacks track forward. Lateral movement beats retreat almost every time.
2. **Ignoring audio.** Players who mute or use speakers instead of headphones miss a whole layer of warning.
3. **Over-committing to combos.** A full string is a commitment. Short strings plus repositioning survive longer.
4. **Skipping the arena scan.** Hazards, ledges, and cover matter more than raw stats.
5. **Treating elites like grunts.** Different rules, different patience requirement.
6. **Never testing defense.** Block windows and dodge invulnerability frames are worth ten minutes of deliberate practice.

If you want to get ahead before launch, replay any action adventure you already own and practice reading telegraphs with the sound on. That skill transfers directly, no matter what the final Winds of Viridia enemy types turn out to be.
