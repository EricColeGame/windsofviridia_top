## The Four Pillars of a Winds of Viridia Boss Strategy

Every decision in a boss arena traces back to one of four tools. Know what each one is for before you walk through the fog door.

**Parry.** Your highest-value defensive option. It converts an enemy attack into an opening, but it demands timing rather than distance. Use it on telegraphed, single-hit swings.

**Vault.** Your escape valve. When a boss commits to a ground-level sweep or a multi-hit combo you can't reliably parry yet, vaulting buys space and resets the exchange.

**Mothbug specials.** Your burst. Save these for windows where the boss is stunned, staggered, or locked into a recovery animation — not as an opener.

**Totems.** Your build. Because totems are combined between battles, a boss you keep losing to is a signal to change your loadout, not just your reflexes.

| Pillar | Job | Best used when | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parry | Convert defense into offense | Attack is single-hit and clearly telegraphed | Whiffing leaves you exposed |
| Vault | Reset spacing | Boss commits to ground-level pressure | Landing poorly near the boss |
| Mothbug special | Burst damage | Boss is staggered or recovering | Wasting it on a shielded phase |
| Totems | Shape the whole fight | Before entering the arena | Over-investing in one gimmick |

## Reading a Boss: A Phase-by-Phase Framework

Most action-adventure bosses follow a three-beat structure, and a phase-by-phase Winds of Viridia boss strategy keeps you from treating the whole fight as one blur.

### Opening phase: learn, don't burn

Spend the first phase collecting information. Watch which attacks come out fast, which ones have a wind-up, and which ones cover the ground you like to stand on. Don't dump your mothbug special yet.

### Pressure phase: find your two punishes

By the middle of the fight you should have identified one attack you can reliably parry and one you can reliably vault past. Those are your damage windows. Everything else is survival.

### Final phase: protect the resource you'll need

Late phases tend to get faster and less forgiving. This is where players burn their special too early and then have nothing for the actual finish. Hold your burst until the boss is visibly staggered, not just damaged.

| Phase | Your goal | What to watch | Mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Gather information | Fast vs. telegraphed attacks | Burning specials immediately |
| Pressure | Identify two reliable punishes | Recovery animations | Greedy third hits |
| Final | Survive and convert staggers | Speed and combo length changes | Empty special meter at the kill window |

## Five Mistakes That Wreck Boss Attempts

Community reports and early player experience with the demo will fill in boss-specific details over the coming months, but the failure patterns below show up in every real-time action game — and every one of them is fixable.

- **Parrying everything.** If a boss throws a three-hit combo, parry the readable hit and vault the rest.
- **Treating the mothbug as a cooldown.** It's a resource. Spend it on staggers, not on the first available moment.
- **Never changing totems.** The game hands you a build system between battles. Use it as a difficulty slider.
- **Ignoring spacing.** Vaulting exists because standing still is a losing plan.
- **Skipping the demo.** Practice reps are free right now.

| Mistake | Why it happens | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Parry spam | Panic under pressure | Block or vault, then parry the last hit |
| Early special usage | Excitement at full meter | Hold until the boss is staggered |
| Static loadout | Forgetting totems exist | Rebuild after every second loss |
| Bad spacing | Chasing damage | Vault out, reset, re-engage |
| No practice | Waiting for launch | Drill in the free demo now |
